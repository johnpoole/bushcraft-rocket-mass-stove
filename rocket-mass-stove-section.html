Rocket mass stove, cut through the middle

All dimensions are in centimetres and measure the inside clear opening: the space the fire and gas travel through. Wall thickness goes outside these numbers, so size your formers to them. Heights are measured from the burn tunnel floor, which is level with the shelter floor.

  • Refractory mix: 1 clay to 1–2 sand, no fibre
  • Light clay insulation: clay slip + sawdust, grass or ash
  • Cob: 1 clay to 2–3 sand, plus fibre; pack dense stone into the bench
  • Flat stone slab (dry-land stone only)
  • Dry stone, under the light clay base and the bench
  • Hollow log, lined inside with clay
  • Fire and exhaust path
