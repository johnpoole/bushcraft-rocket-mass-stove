PLUZ rules that shape this camp

  • Temporary only. Everything you bring goes out with you, and everything you build comes apart and goes back where it came from. No cutting live trees.
  • Check the stove first. The general rules say fires go in existing fire pits or portable fire receptacles, and that any structure needs approval from Alberta Environment and Protected Areas. A cob stove you take apart may still count, so call the Calgary district office before you build it.
  • At least 30 m from the creek. The pick itself is closer than that, so you'll set up a little back from it.
  • 14 days max in one spot, then move at least 1 km for 72 hours.
  • Fires only for cooking or warmth, never during a fire ban, never unattended, and out until cold to the touch.
  • Toilet at least 70 m from water, in a hole at least 15 cm deep.
Rules summarised from alberta.ca on 8 Oct 2026. Check the fire ban map before every trip.
