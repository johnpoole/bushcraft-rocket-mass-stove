PLUZ rules for this area

  • Camping gear must be portable and temporary. Any structure or improvement to the land needs prior approval from Alberta Environment and Protected Areas.
  • Fires only for cooking or warmth, in existing fire pits or portable fire receptacles, never during a fire ban, never unattended, and out until cold to the touch.
  • Camp at least 30 m from lakes and streams.
  • 14 days max in one spot, then move at least 1 km for 72 hours.
  • Toilet at least 70 m from water, in a hole at least 15 cm deep.
